LEVANTE offline launcher — Ghana demo

For Chrissy and Amy · Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ and Mac laptops

This is a demo of the dev launcher on the Samsung tablet. Use the practice roster already in the dev project. The Macs are for checking that roster and, after the demo, showing that the results uploaded.

The current launcher link stops working on 19 October 2026. If the demo is on or after that date, tell David before you fly so the link can be republished.

Take with you

  • The Galaxy Tab A9+, charger, and a cable.
  • Both Macs. Either one can open the dashboard.
  • Headphones. Several tasks play audio.
  • The researcher email and password for the dev project (hs-levante-admin-dev). The same account is used on the Mac and on the tablet. A site admin or research assistant account is enough.
  • The name of the demo site, the assignment, and the school, classroom, or cohort.

Children never sign in. A fresh tablet has no device PIN.

The night before you fly

Do this once on good Wi-Fi, on the tablet you will carry.

  1. On a Mac, open the dashboard: https://hs-levante-admin-dev.web.app. Sign in. Confirm the demo site has children (each with a birth month and birth year) and an assignment attached to a school, classroom, or cohort. An assignment that is only attached to the site cannot be downloaded.
  2. On the tablet, open Chrome. Samsung Internet will not work for this. Go to https://hs-levante-admin-dev--offline-launcher-34g4znyg.web.app
  3. Chrome menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen → Install.
  4. Open the new Home screen icon. The page should say Opened from Home screen.
  5. 1 · Select Site. Sign in. If Continue with Google leaves the app, use email and password, then reopen the Home screen icon. Tap the demo site, then Continue to provision.
  6. 2 · Provision. Tap the assignment and group (the line under the name says the school, classroom, or cohort). Tap Download pack. Leave the tablet on Wi-Fi, screen on, until the pack status is ready. A pack is often tens of megabytes and can take a few minutes.
  7. 3 · Collect Data. Tap Start child mode. Tap one practice child, then one short task, and play it through. Turn Wi-Fi off and play a second task. Turn Wi-Fi back on.
  8. Tap On-site Researcher at the bottom, then Exit child mode.
  9. 4 · Sync. Sign in again. Tap Sync. On the Mac, refresh the assignment in the dashboard and confirm that child shows progress.

Set the tablet’s screen timeout to 30 minutes so a task is not killed mid-demo. Leave a few hundred megabytes of storage free.

Demo day

On the tablet, online, only if the pack is not already ready. Open the Home screen icon, 1 · Select Site, then 2 · Provision, then Download pack. Stay on Wi-Fi until the status is ready. A pack downloaded in Chrome on a Mac stays on that Mac. The tablet has to download its own.

Collect with the network off. Open the Home screen icon. On 3 · Collect Data, confirm it says Opened from Home screen and the pack name is the one you expect. Tap Start child mode. Turn on airplane mode, then turn Wi-Fi off as well (Samsung often leaves Wi-Fi on). Hand the tablet to someone:

  1. They tap their own name.
  2. They tap a task.
  3. When it ends, the name list returns.
  4. The next person taps a different name.

You do not sign in or download again between people. A checkmark means that task was already finished. It can still be played again. Leave child mode only when the demo is over: On-site Researcher → Exit child mode. That also saves a JSON file into the tablet’s Downloads folder. That file is a spare copy. It is not the upload.

Show the upload on a Mac. Turn Wi-Fi back on. On the tablet, open 4 · Sync, sign in, and tap Sync N pending run(s). Wait until those rows say synced. On the Mac, open the same assignment in the dashboard and show the completed tasks.

If something stalls

  • No assignments for this site. The account cannot see that site, or the assignment has no school, classroom, or cohort. Fix it on the Mac dashboard, then on the tablet choose the site again.
  • A child will not start. Their profile is missing birth month or birth year. Add both on the Mac, then download the pack again.
  • Download stops. Stay on 2 · Provision and use Resume on that pack. Do not delete it unless you are ready to start the download over.
  • A task fails immediately. Go back to the roster and pick a different task. Theory of Mind is a known failure; use another task for the demo.
  • The Home screen icon asks you to sign in and then shows a missing page. The preview link has expired (19 October 2026).
  • You cleared Chrome’s data, or uninstalled the Home screen app. The pack and any runs not yet synced are gone. Download the pack again. Synced runs are already on the server.

Keep using the Home screen icon for the whole trip. Signing out of the researcher account is fine. Wiping the tablet, clearing Chrome site data, or removing the installed app deletes the local pack.
